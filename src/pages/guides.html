What we deliberately left out

You will not find a packing list of forty items here, or a list of "hidden gems" that every second blog already publishes. We also skip hotel rankings: a place that suits a family in February may be a poor choice for a solo traveller in July, and a list would pretend otherwise. Where a guide names an area or a street, it is because the area genuinely changes your day, not because anyone paid for the mention.

Reviewed in September 2026. Travel conditions in Vietnam change — a road is upgraded, a ferry schedule moves, a visa rule is adjusted. Every guide carries the date we last checked it, and corrections are welcome through the about page.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I plan a Vietnam trip?

Two to four months is comfortable for peak season from October to April: enough time for cheaper flights, the boat cabin you want and your first choice of hotel. In the shoulder months you can plan a trip in a few weeks and still travel well, especially if you stay flexible about the order of your stops.

Is Vietnam easy to travel independently?

Yes. Domestic flights are frequent and cheap, ride-hailing apps work in every city, English is common in hotels and tour offices, and guesthouses are used to independent travellers. The two things worth arranging ahead are the overnight boat in the north and transport during Tet, when the country moves and seats sell out.

How much English do I need to get by?

None at all in hotels, airports, restaurants in tourist areas and with guides. Outside those, a translation app and a few Vietnamese words go a long way, and pointing at a dish someone else is eating is a perfectly respectable ordering strategy. Download an offline language pack before you fly.
