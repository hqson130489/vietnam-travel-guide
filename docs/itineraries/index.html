Vietnam Itineraries: 5, 7 and 10 Day Routes

Updated September 2026 · 703 words

Three itineraries, built for the three trip lengths most first-time visitors actually have. Each one is written day by day, with honest transfer times, an area-by-area note on where to sleep, and a budget table at three comfort levels. Pick the one closest to your dates and adjust from there.

  • 5 days in northern Vietnam A practical five-day northern route through Hanoi, Ninh Binh and a Lan Ha Bay overnight boat, with travel times and costs. Hanoi · Ninh Binh · Lan Ha Bay
  • 7 days from Hanoi to Hoi An A day-by-day 7-day route from Hanoi and Ninh Binh to Hoi An and Hue, with travel times, area-by-area stays and a three-tier budget table. Hanoi · Ninh Binh · Hoi An · Hue
  • 10 days from north to south A ten-day first-timer route from Hanoi and Lan Ha Bay to Hoi An, Hue and Ho Chi Minh City, with two flights and a Mekong day trip. Hanoi · Lan Ha Bay · Hoi An · Saigon · Mekong

How to choose without overthinking it

  • Under seven days, stay in one region. Two internal flights in a short trip cost you most of a day each once you count the airport time, and the north alone has enough for a week.
  • Seven to nine days is the sweet spot for two regions. Fly the long leg once — Hanoi to Da Nang, for example — and keep everything else on the ground.
  • Ten days is the minimum for north to south. Below that you spend more time in transit than in the places you came to see.
  • Book the overnight boat first. Lan Ha and Ha Long Bay cabins are the least flexible part of any northern route, especially from October to April.

Every itinerary here can be run independently, and all of them can be shortened. The day-by-day notes flag which day to drop first if your dates shrink by 24 hours.

Frequently asked questions

How many places should I visit in a week in Vietnam?

Two bases is comfortable, three is busy, four is a packing drill. Each move costs you half a day once you count checkout, the transfer itself and settling in. Most people enjoy a seven-day trip more with two bases and a day trip than with four stops and no slack.

Should I travel north to south or south to north?

Either works. North to south suits most first-timers because Hanoi is the calmer introduction and the trip builds towards Ho Chi Minh City's energy. Flying into Hanoi and home from Ho Chi Minh City — an open-jaw ticket — saves a full day compared with returning north to catch a flight.

Are these itineraries suitable for families with young children?

Yes, with two adjustments. Cut the number of moves, because children handle one long transfer far better than three short ones, and keep the overnight boat to a single night. The five-day northern route is the easiest starting point, and Ninh Binh's boat rides and easy cycling are the most child-friendly days in it.

Do I need to book everything before I arrive?

Book the overnight boats, any domestic flights and your first two nights. Everything else can wait until you are on the ground, which keeps you flexible if a typhoon or a change of mood rearranges the plan. Guesthouses and small hotels in the shoulder season usually have room at short notice.
