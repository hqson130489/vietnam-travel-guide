About This Guide

Updated September 2026 · 657 words

Vietnam Travel Guide is a small, independent planning resource: three itineraries, one budget tool and a handful of guides, written by people who live in Vietnam and run trips here for a living. It is not a booking site, and nothing on it is for sale.

Who is behind it

The guide is published by Vibe Vietnam Tour, the travel brand of Wingolf Joint Stock Company, an international tour operator licensed in Vietnam (licence No. 01-611/2024/TCDL-GP LHQT, tax code 0109910007). The team is based in Hanoi, at Số 7, Ngõ 26/1 Phố Đỗ Quang, Phường Yên Hoà, and works with drivers, guides and boat crews across the north, centre and south of the country.

That matters for one reason: the routes here are the routes our own drivers actually drive, and the timings are the ones we plan around when a guest has a flight to catch. We also see the mistakes first-time visitors make, because we answer those emails every week.

How the content is put together

  • Written separately from our tour pages. Nothing on this site is copied from vibevietnamtour.com, and nothing here is a rewritten sales page. The itineraries are written to be run independently, with or without us.
  • No paid placements. No hotel, cruise or booking platform pays to appear in these guides. Where we name a street or an area, it is a planning decision, not a commercial one.
  • Prices are ranges, dated and reviewed. Every money figure carries the month and year we checked it, and the guides explain what makes the real price move — season, holidays and how far ahead you book.
  • No invented numbers. If we cannot verify a figure, we leave it out or label it clearly as an estimate. We do not publish awards, review counts or statistics we cannot point to.

About the prices on this site

All figures are planning estimates in US dollars, reviewed in September 2026. They are compiled from what our own travellers pay for hotels, meals, transport and activities in a normal week. Peak dates — Tet, the late-April public holiday and the Christmas-to-New-Year stretch — can push hotel rates well above the ranges shown, and booking late almost always costs more. Treat the numbers as a way to size your budget, not as a quote.

What this site does not do: it does not sell tours, take bookings, collect email addresses, set cookies or run analytics. The budget calculator works inside your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

What we do for a living

When we are not writing this guide, the same team designs and operates private Vietnam tours and tailor-made itineraries — day tours in Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Hoi An and Hue, Ha Long Bay and Lan Ha Bay cruises, and multi-day routes across the country. If you would rather hand the whole plan to someone local, that is the door to knock on. If you would rather build it yourself, everything you need is on this site, free.

Corrections and questions

If something here is out of date, wrong, or simply unclear, tell us and we will fix it — a road changes, a ferry timetable shifts, a price band moves. The quickest route is the contact page on the main site, where the message reaches the same team that writes these pages.

For a quote, a custom route or a question about an existing booking, use the Vibe Vietnam Tour contact page. For anything about the content of this guide, mention "travel guide" in your message so it is routed to us rather than to the sales desk.
